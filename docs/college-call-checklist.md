# College phone-survey checklist

A reference for what to capture when calling each college (or
researching it manually). The site already has the basic identity
(name, district, type, fee band, course categories) for all 382
colleges. This document covers everything **else** that's worth
collecting before you trust a college's listing.

Each item has:
- ☐ a checkbox to tick as you collect it
- A note on **why it matters** to the student
- A **fallback source** if the call doesn't yield it

Print one of these per college, or copy into a spreadsheet — one row
per college, one column per item.

---

## A. Identity verification (5 min before the call)

Confirm these are right before you dial — saves embarrassment.

- ☐ **Full official name** — exactly as written on the college's own letterhead.
  *Why:* mis-naming a college in the database is a credibility kill.
  *Fallback:* their website's `<title>` tag or the KTU / DCE list.
- ☐ **Affiliating university** — KTU, MG University, University of Calicut, Kannur, Kerala, CUSAT, etc.
  *Why:* drives which entrance exam + which calendar the student follows.
  *Fallback:* affiliated-college list on each university's site.
- ☐ **Year established**
  *Why:* "the new college that opened in 2018" reads very differently from "the 1924 government college".
  *Fallback:* About page on the website, or NIRF report.
- ☐ **Funding type** — govt / aided / private-self-financing / minority-aided / CAPE (govt cost-sharing)
  *Why:* changes the cost expectations dramatically.
  *Fallback:* Kerala DCE list categorises every college.

---

## B. Contact details

These go into our `College` record as `phone`, `admissionsEmail`, `website`.

- ☐ **Main reception phone** — the number on the official website.
- ☐ **Admissions office direct line** — if different. Mark which is for which.
- ☐ **WhatsApp number** — many Kerala colleges run admissions via WhatsApp now.
- ☐ **Admissions email** — `admissions@…`, not the generic `info@`.
- ☐ **Principal's office email** — for escalation; don't publish unless permitted.
- ☐ **Website URL** — must be the canonical `.ac.in` if they have one.
- ☐ **Physical address + Google Maps link**
- ☐ **Office hours** — when can a student call back during admissions?

---

## C. Programmes + seats

For our site this is the most important section. **Get the full B.Tech
branch list with seat counts**, not just "they offer engineering".

- ☐ **Each B.Tech branch** (or BBA / BCA / BCom / B.Sc / B.Pharm / MBBS / etc.)
  - Full official branch name (e.g. "Electronics and Computer Engineering", not "ECE")
  - Total seats / intake per year
  - Number going to each quota (merit / management / NRI for private; merit only for govt)
- ☐ **PG programmes** — note in passing; not the primary audience but useful.
- ☐ **Last year's last-rank-allotted** per branch (if they'll share)
  *Why:* gives the student a realistic admit-chance read.
  *Fallback:* KEAM "Last Rank Table" PDF on cee.kerala.gov.in.
- ☐ **Specialisations / electives within branches** — e.g. CSE with AI/ML elective track.

---

## D. Fees + financials

The hardest part to get reliably. Ask politely and write down whatever they share.

- ☐ **Tuition fee per year — by quota:**
  - Merit / KEAM allotted: ₹___
  - Management quota: ₹___
  - NRI quota: ₹___
- ☐ **Hostel fees per year** (separate boys / girls if different)
- ☐ **Mess fees per year**
- ☐ **One-time admission / caution / development fees** — broken out
- ☐ **University fees** — usually separate from college fees
- ☐ **Lab / equipment fees** if any
- ☐ **Exam fees** per semester
- ☐ **Total approximate first-year out-of-pocket** — sum of above
- ☐ **Scholarships available** — name, eligibility, value
- ☐ **EWS / SC / ST / OEC waivers** — what % of fees are waived
- ☐ **Education loan tie-ups** — banks they have arrangements with

---

## E. Hostel + living

Big factor for students from far districts.

- ☐ Hostel available? Boys / Girls / Both
- ☐ Approximate capacity
- ☐ On-campus vs separate building
- ☐ Veg-only mess or both
- ☐ Visitor policy
- ☐ Curfew time (it's Kerala — there often is one)
- ☐ Off-campus PG options nearby — rough rent range
- ☐ Distance to nearest railway station / bus stand

---

## F. Admissions process

What the student actually needs to do.

- ☐ Which entrance exams are accepted — KEAM, JEE, etc.
- ☐ Direct-admission pathway? (for management quota usually yes)
- ☐ Application window — when does it open / close?
- ☐ Application fee
- ☐ Documents required (10th + 12th mark sheets, TC, conduct cert, EWS cert, …)
- ☐ Counselling rounds — state-level + college-level
- ☐ Last date to confirm seat after allotment
- ☐ Withdrawal refund policy

---

## G. Quality signals

The "is this a good college" indicators we'd want to surface.

- ☐ **NAAC grade** — A++, A+, A, B++, B+, B, C, or "not yet accredited"
- ☐ **NIRF rank** — overall + by category (Engineering / University / etc.)
- ☐ **NBA accreditation** — per branch (for engineering)
- ☐ **AICTE approval number** — for engineering / management
- ☐ **Placement rate last year** — % of final-year students placed
- ☐ **Average package** offered last year
- ☐ **Highest package** last year
- ☐ **Top 3 recruiters** last year
- ☐ **Industry partnerships** — Infosys / TCS internships, etc.
- ☐ **Number of student startups** if they track that

---

## H. Campus life

Texture, not data. Optional but useful for the college's detail page.

- ☐ Total student strength on campus
- ☐ Faculty-student ratio
- ☐ Notable annual fest (name + month)
- ☐ Sports facilities (cricket / football grounds, gym, swimming pool)
- ☐ Library size / 24-hour reading hall?
- ☐ Wi-Fi coverage
- ☐ Cafeteria options + average meal cost

---

## I. Safety + reputation

What a parent would ask. Easier to verify from news + alumni than from the college itself.

- ☐ Any well-known safety incidents in the last 3 years?
- ☐ Anti-ragging committee — yes (everyone says yes, note their helpline number)
- ☐ Hostel security setup (CCTV, watchmen, gate-pass system)
- ☐ Distance to nearest hospital
- ☐ Reputation snapshot — what alumni say on Quora / LinkedIn / Reddit

---

## J. Verification

The last 30 seconds of the call.

- ☐ Name + designation of the person you spoke to — for follow-up
- ☐ Date of the call
- ☐ Permission to publish their numbers on our site — yes / no
- ☐ Best time to call back during admission season

---

## Tips for the calls

- Best time: **10:30 AM – 12:30 PM, Monday–Friday**. Avoid late afternoon (admin staff often leave by 4) and avoid first week of June (chaos season).
- Open with: "Hi, I'm building a free directory for Kerala 12th-pass students. Could I check a few details about your college?" — frames you as helpful, not selling.
- If they push back on fee numbers, ask for the **prospectus PDF** instead — most colleges email it on request.
- Always note **when** you collected each number (a "verified May 2026" stamp ages gracefully). Re-verify annually.
- If they refuse to share a particular number, mark it as "—" not blank. Distinguishes "didn't ask" from "asked and they wouldn't say".
