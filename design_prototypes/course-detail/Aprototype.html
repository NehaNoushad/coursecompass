Overview

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering is a four-year undergraduate degree that turns you into someone who can design, build, and reason about software systems. The first two years build your mathematical and programming foundation — discrete mathematics, data structures, algorithms, computer organisation. The final two years branch into specialisations: operating systems, networks, databases, AI/ML, and a major project where you ship something real.

It is consistently the most-applied-to engineering branch in Kerala, and for good reason: the job market for CSE graduates stretches from Infopark Kochi to Bengaluru to the Bay Area. Kerala's strong IT sector (TCS, Infosys, UST, IBS, and dozens of product companies) recruits heavily from state engineering colleges, so a local B.Tech CSE opens genuine doors — you don't need to relocate on day one unless you want to.

Entrance exams

You need to clear at least one of these to get an admission offer. Many students appear for both to widen their options.

KEAM
KEAM

Full name

Conducted by

April – May

Medium
Scope
Kerala state colleges only
What it unlocks: All Kerala Engineering colleges under LBS counselling — including CET, GEC Thrissur, TKM, Barton Hill, and all government-aided engineering colleges. Rank + marks determines your allotment.
