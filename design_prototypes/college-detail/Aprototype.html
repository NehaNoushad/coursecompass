Overview
About Sacred Heart College

Founded by the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (CMI) in 1944 and located on the scenic banks of the Vembanad Lake in Thevara, Ernakulam, Sacred Heart College is one of Kerala's most academically decorated institutions. The college received the UGC's Autonomous status — a mark of independent academic excellence — and has maintained a NAAC A++ accreditation, placing it in the top tier of Indian arts and sciences colleges.

The college is particularly well known for its English Literature, Economics, Mathematics, and Physics departments, whose faculty have a strong research publication record. Students benefit from a structured mentorship programme, an active placement cell, and a campus culture that balances academic rigour with a vibrant extracurricular scene. The annual intercollegiate arts and cultural festival draws participants from across Kerala.

With around 2,100 seats across undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, and aided-college fee structures that keep costs accessible, Sacred Heart consistently ranks in the NIRF national top-100 — reaching #57 in the most recent listing. It is affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam, while exercising full autonomy in curriculum design and examinations.

Admissions
How to apply
Application window
Typically May – June each year, through the Kerala CAP (Centralised Admissions Process) portal. Some management quota seats are filled directly by the college. Check the official website for this year's opening date.
Entrance exams accepted
For UG admission to aided-college seats, KMAT (Kerala Management Aptitude Test) and CUET scores are considered where applicable. Arts, Science, and Commerce UG seats are primarily merit-based (Class 12 marks).
CUET KMAT Class 12 merit
How to apply
Register on the Kerala Higher Education admission portal (hscap.kerala.gov.in), submit your Class 12 mark sheet and community certificate. Admission lists are published on the college notice board and website. No separate entrance test for most UG programmes.
