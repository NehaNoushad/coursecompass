About the college

Sacred Heart College

Perched on the banks of the Vembanad Lake in Thevara, Ernakulam, Sacred Heart College was founded in 1944 by the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate and has spent eight decades building a reputation that holds up under national scrutiny. Twice accredited at the highest grade by NAAC, and a regular feature inside NIRF's national top-60 for arts and sciences colleges, it is the kind of institution where its alumni list and its entrance queue both reflect genuine aspiration.

"A regular feature in the NIRF top-60 — known equally for its lakefront setting and its Mathematics and English Lit departments."
— Paper Plane profile

The college holds UGC Autonomous status, which means it designs its own syllabi, sets its own question papers, and awards its own certificates — a meaningful academic freedom that lets departments modernise faster than affiliating-university cycles allow. Affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam, for formal degree conferment, but the day-to-day academic life runs to the college's own academic calendar and research priorities.

With around 2,100 seats spread across undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, it is large enough for genuine peer diversity but compact enough that individual faculty-student mentoring is part of the institutional culture rather than a prospectus promise. An active placement cell, strong industry linkages in Ernakulam's commerce and IT corridor, and a campus that hosts one of Kerala's better-known intercollegiate cultural festivals complete the picture.
